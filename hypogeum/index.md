A hypogeum is an underground chamber, the vault beneath a temple or a villa. As above, so below: the seven rooms hold what is alive, and this one keeps everything that has finished living.

## Rules of the vault

- **Nothing is deleted.** Finished projects, ended areas, outgrown resources, and abandoned ideas descend here.
- **The original room is kept.** A project from the Atelier is filed at `hypogeum/atelier/…`.
- **Every descent is dated and explained.** The note gains `archived` and `reason` fields.
- **Anything can rise again.** Ask Claude `Where is …?` and it will search the vault too.

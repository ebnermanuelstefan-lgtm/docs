The seven principles below come from *The Kybalion* (1908), signed by "Three Initiates" and usually attributed to William Walker Atkinson. It is a modern synthesis, not a text of the ancient *Corpus Hermeticum*. Its seven formulations remain a precise design language, so each one here governs a mechanism of the palace.

| Principle | Formula | Mechanism in the palace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mentalism** | "The All is Mind." | The palace is a model of your mind. What is not captured does not exist for it, so capture is frictionless and constant. |
| **Correspondence** | "As above, so below." | Seven rooms above, one below. The Speculum mirrors you, and the palace's structure mirrors your life. |
| **Vibration** | "Nothing rests; everything moves." | Every note has a status and moves: seed to evergreen, active to done. Stale notes surface at reviews. |
| **Polarity** | "Everything is dual; opposites are identical in nature, but different in degree." | The counterweight. On the dimensions you choose, Claude takes the other pole of your mind. |
| **Rhythm** | "The measure of the swing to the right is the measure of the swing to the left." | The rituals: daily capture, weekly review, the lunar cycle, the seasons. Expansion and pruning alternate. |
| **Cause and effect** | "Every cause has its effect; every effect has its cause." | The decision journal. You record your reasoning before the outcome, and time grades it. |
| **Gender** | "Gender is in everything." | Two modes of work: receptive (collecting in the Atrium and Bibliotheca) and generative (creating in the Hortus and Atelier). A healthy week contains both. |

## Polarity, in practice

Polarity is the principle that answers your request: a mind that both reflects yours and is its opposite. The Kybalion's claim is that opposites are degrees of one thing, as heat and cold are degrees of temperature. A counterweight is therefore not a contrarian. It is the missing half of the same mind.

The palace applies this through one rule: **mirror the form, counterweight the function.** Claude writes in your voice and honours your values, while thinking against your defaults where you ask it to. The [Speculum](/speculum) explains how.

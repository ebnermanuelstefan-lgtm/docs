A second brain fails in one way: it stops being visited. The rituals below are short on purpose. Claude runs each one with you. You only have to start it.

## The rhythm

| Ritual | When | Time | You say | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Capture | Any moment | 10 seconds | `Capture: …` | The thought lands in the Atrium, timestamped. Nothing else. |
| Daily note | Morning or evening | 5 minutes | `Daily` | Three prompts: the one thing that matters, what you noticed, what carries over. |
| Weekly review | Once a week, same day | 25 minutes | `Weekly review` | The Atrium is emptied, projects get next actions, ideas are harvested, and the counterweight reports. |
| Lunar review | New moon and full moon | 30 minutes each | `Lunar review` | New moon: choose one to three focuses. Full moon: harvest what ripened, release what did not. |
| Seasonal review | Equinoxes and solstices | 1 hour | `Seasonal review` | You and Claude reread the Speculum and retune the polarity map. |

The lunar cycle lasts about 29.5 days, which makes it a natural monthly measure with two built-in turning points. If you prefer the calendar, say so; the reviews move to the first and fifteenth of the month.

## The weekly review

The weekly review is the heart of the system. In order:

1. **Empty the Atrium.** Every item is filed, merged, or discarded.
2. **Walk the Atelier.** Each active project has one concrete next action, or it is paused.
3. **Walk the Tablinum.** Any area below its standard is named.
4. **Harvest the Ephemeris.** Ideas buried in the week's daily notes are planted in the Hortus.
5. **Grade decisions.** Decisions whose review date has arrived are graded in the journal.
6. **Counterweight report.** Claude names three patterns it noticed this week and asks one uncomfortable question.
7. **Choose.** You name the one thing that matters next week.

## Starting points

Claude's suggestions to begin the shelf. Keep, replace, or delete them.

- **Baldassare Castiglione, *Il libro del cortegiano*** (1528). The origin of *sprezzatura*: mastery worn so lightly it looks effortless.
- **Jean Anthelme Brillat-Savarin, *Physiologie du goût*** (1825). Taste treated as a science and an art at once: "Tell me what you eat, and I will tell you what you are."
- **Epicurus, *Letter to Menoeceus***. The counterweight on this shelf: pleasure understood as tranquillity and the absence of disturbance, not as abundance.

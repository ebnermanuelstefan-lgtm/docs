## Core questions

- What makes something worth owning, and worth keeping?
- How do craft, provenance, and restraint define real luxury?
- How can daily rituals carry beauty?

## Domains

- **Craft and maisons:** watchmaking, tailoring (Savile Row, Neapolitan), leather goods, perfumery.
- **The table:** wine regions (Burgundy, Bordeaux, Champagne, Melnik and the Struma valley), gastronomy, the art of hosting.
- **The home:** interiors, objects, books, light.
- **Travel:** grand hotels, slow journeys, cities to return to.
- **Rituals:** morning, tea and coffee, reading, dressing.

## Reading

- Jean Anthelme Brillat-Savarin, *Physiologie du goût*.
- Baldassare Castiglione, *Il Cortegiano*, on *sprezzatura*.
- Richard Sennett, *The Craftsman*.
- Hugh Johnson and Jancis Robinson, *The World Atlas of Wine*.

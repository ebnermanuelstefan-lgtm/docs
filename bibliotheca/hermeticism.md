## Core questions

- What does "as above, so below" mean in practice?
- How did Hermetic ideas travel through late antiquity, the Renaissance, and modern esotericism?
- Where do Hermeticism, alchemy, and astrology overlap, and where do they part?

## Primary texts

- The *Corpus Hermeticum*, especially the *Poimandres*.
- The *Asclepius*.
- The *Emerald Tablet* (*Tabula Smaragdina*).
- Marsilio Ficino's Latin translation (1463, printed 1471) and *De vita libri tres*.

## Scholarship

- Frances Yates, *Giordano Bruno and the Hermetic Tradition*.
- Brian Copenhaver, *Hermetica*.
- Antoine Faivre, *Access to Western Esotericism*.
- Wouter Hanegraaff, *Western Esotericism: A Guide for the Perplexed*.
- *The Kybalion* (1908): a modern New Thought synthesis, not an ancient text. The palace's [seven principles](/palace/principles) draw on it.

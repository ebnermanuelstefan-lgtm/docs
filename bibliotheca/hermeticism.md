## Starting points

Claude's suggestions to begin the shelf. Keep, replace, or delete them.

- **Brian Copenhaver, *Hermetica*** (1992). The standard scholarly translation of the *Corpus Hermeticum* and the *Asclepius*, with notes.
- **Frances Yates, *The Art of Memory*** (1966). The history of the memory palace from Simonides to Bruno, and the foundation stone of this one.
- **Antoine Faivre, *Accès de l'ésotérisme occidental*** (1986; in English as *Access to Western Esotericism*, 1994). The French scholar who defined Western esotericism as a field of study.

## Starting points

Claude's suggestions to begin the shelf. Keep, replace, or delete them.

- **Vitruvius, *De architectura*** (about 30–15 BC). The triad of *firmitas*, *utilitas*, and *venustas* (strength, use, and beauty) that every later theory answers.
- **Andrea Palladio, *I quattro libri dell'architettura*** (1570). Proportion made practical, and the source of Palladianism from England to Virginia.
- **Rudolf Wittkower, *Architectural Principles in the Age of Humanism*** (1949). How the Renaissance tied harmonic proportion in buildings to music and the cosmos.

## Core questions

- What makes a building endure, in matter and in meaning?
- How do proportion, light, and material shape the way a space is felt?
- Where do classical order and modern freedom meet?

## Canon

- Vitruvius, *De architectura*: *firmitas, utilitas, venustas* (strength, utility, beauty).
- Leon Battista Alberti, *De re aedificatoria*.
- Andrea Palladio, *I quattro libri dell'architettura*.
- Claude-Nicolas Ledoux, *L'Architecture considérée sous le rapport de l'art, des mœurs et de la législation*.
- Le Corbusier, *Vers une architecture*, and the Modulor.
- Christopher Alexander, *A Pattern Language* and *The Timeless Way of Building*.
- Peter Zumthor, *Atmospheres* and *Thinking Architecture*.

## Threads to follow

- The classical orders: Tuscan, Doric, Ionic, Corinthian, Composite.
- Palladian villas and their afterlife in English country houses.
- Bulgarian Revival architecture: Plovdiv Old Town, Koprivshtitsa, the Rila Monastery.
- Beaux-Arts Paris and Haussmann's boulevards.
- Geometry in the Gothic cathedral: Chartres, Reims.

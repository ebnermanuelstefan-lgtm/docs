## Core questions

- How did traditional (Hellenistic, medieval) astrology differ from modern psychological astrology?
- What do I actually observe, as opposed to what I am told to expect?
- How can astrological cycles serve as a rhythm for reflection?

## Canon

- Claudius Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos*.
- Vettius Valens, *Anthology*.
- William Lilly, *Christian Astrology*.
- Dane Rudhyar, *The Astrology of Personality*.
- Liz Greene, *Saturn: A New Look at an Old Devil*.
- Chris Brennan, *Hellenistic Astrology*.

## Cycles the palace keeps

- The lunar cycle: new moon and full moon reviews, see [Rituals](/palace/rituals).
- The seasons: equinoxes and solstices for the seasonal review.
- The long cycles: Jupiter and Saturn returns.

Birth data stays out of the palace while the repository is public.

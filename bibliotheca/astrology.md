## Starting points

Claude's suggestions to begin the shelf. Keep, replace, or delete them.

- **Claudius Ptolemy, *Tetrabiblos*** (2nd century AD). The founding textbook of Western astrology, written by the astronomer of the *Almagest*.
- **Nicholas Campion, *A History of Western Astrology*** (two volumes, 2008–2009). The tradition traced from Mesopotamia to the present, as cultural history.
- **Richard Tarnas, *Cosmos and Psyche*** (2006). The archetypal school: planetary cycles read as patterns of meaning in history and culture.

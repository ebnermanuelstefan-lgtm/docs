## Starting points

Claude's suggestions to begin the shelf. Keep, replace, or delete them.

- **Giorgio Vasari, *Lives of the Most Excellent Painters, Sculptors, and Architects*** (1550, enlarged 1568). The book that invented art history, and the Renaissance's portrait of itself.
- **Erwin Panofsky, *Studies in Iconology*** (1939). A method for reading the symbols in an image, from what it shows to what it means.
- **John Berger, *Ways of Seeing*** (1972). The counterweight on this shelf: a direct challenge to how the classical canon asks to be looked at.

## Core questions

- What does a work of art ask of its viewer?
- How do technique, patronage, and belief shape a period's style?
- What separates the timeless from the fashionable?

## Canon

- Giorgio Vasari, *Lives of the Most Excellent Painters, Sculptors, and Architects*.
- Heinrich Wölfflin, *Principles of Art History*.
- Ernst Gombrich, *The Story of Art*.
- Kenneth Clark, *Civilisation*.
- John Berger, *Ways of Seeing*.
- Wassily Kandinsky, *Concerning the Spiritual in Art*.

## Periods and movements

- Byzantine and Orthodox iconography, including the Tarnovo school.
- The Italian Renaissance: Florence, Venice, Rome.
- The Northern Renaissance: van Eyck, Dürer.
- The Baroque: Caravaggio, Bernini, Rubens.
- French academic painting and Impressionism.
- Symbolism: Moreau, Redon, the Nabis.

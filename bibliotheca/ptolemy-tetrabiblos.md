# Tetrabiblos, Claudius Ptolemy

## Summary in three sentences
Ptolemy wrote this companion to the *Almagest* in second-century Alexandria. In it he separates astronomy, certain knowledge of the heavens' motions, from astrology, probable knowledge of their effects that is no less real than medicine or navigation. He explains planetary influence "physically" through hot, cold, wet and dry, and he covers both the fate of peoples and the individual nativity. Its systematic, naturalistic approach made it the standard astrological textbook for some fifteen centuries.

> **BG:** Основният учебник по астрология от Античността до Новото време. Птолемей разграничава точната астрономия от вероятностната астрология и обяснява планетите чрез топло, студено, влажно и сухо.

## Key ideas
1. **Book I: foundations.** A defence of astrology as possible and useful. The planetary natures come from the four qualities: the Sun heats and dries, the Moon moistens, Saturn chills, Mars burns. Benefics and malefics, sect, aspects, triplicities, rulerships and terms. The zodiac is anchored to the equinoxes and solstices (tropical).
2. **Book II: mundane astrology.** Regions and peoples under the signs, eclipses as the chief signal of collective events, and weather.
3. **Books III–IV: nativities.** Parents, siblings, length of life, body, quality of soul, fortune, action, marriage, children, travel and death.
4. **Judgment by combination.** Weigh several significators together, never one factor alone.
5. **A naturalistic choice.** Ptolemy leaves aside much of the lot-based and divinatory technique of contemporaries such as Vettius Valens.

## Quotations

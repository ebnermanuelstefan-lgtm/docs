♀︎ **Venus's room: beauty and the arts.** The Bibliotheca holds what you collect from the world. What you *think* about it grows in the [Hortus](/hortus).

Say `Read: …`, `Прочетено: …`, or `Lu : …` to log a source on the right shelf from `_templates/source.md`. Sources move from `unread` to `reading` to `distilled`.

## Cross-currents

The shelves lean on each other. These threads run through several of them.

- **Proportion and harmony:** Vitruvius, Pythagorean ratios, Palladio, the musical intervals.
- **Sacred geometry:** temples and cathedrals, and the Hermetic correspondence of macrocosm and microcosm.
- **Taste as philosophy:** Hume's *Of the Standard of Taste*, Kant's *Critique of Judgment*, the art of living well.
- **Time and cycles:** astrological houses, liturgical calendars, seasonal living.

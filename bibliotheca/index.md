The library holds what others made: books, lectures, buildings visited, paintings seen, quotations, and references. What you think about them is planted in the [Hortus](/hortus).

## How a source enters

Tell Claude `Read: …` with the title, author, or a link. Claude opens a source note on the right shelf with four parts: the source in one sentence, what struck you, the quotations worth keeping, and your response. Your response is where Hortus seeds come from.

Source notes move from `unread` to `reading` to `distilled`.

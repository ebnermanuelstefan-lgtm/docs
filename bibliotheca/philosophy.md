## Core questions

- How should one live?
- What can be known, and how?
- How are the beautiful, the good, and the true related?

## Canon

- Plato: *Symposium*, *Republic*, *Timaeus*.
- Aristotle: *Nicomachean Ethics*, *Poetics*, *Metaphysics*.
- The Stoics: Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations*; Seneca, *Letters to Lucilius*; Epictetus, *Enchiridion*.
- Plotinus, *Enneads*.
- Montaigne, *Essais*.
- Spinoza, *Ethics*.
- Kant, *Critique of Judgment*.
- Nietzsche, *The Gay Science*.
- Pierre Hadot, *Philosophy as a Way of Life*.

## Threads to follow

- Neoplatonism and its path into Renaissance thought: Ficino, Pico della Mirandola.
- Aesthetics, from Plato's Forms to Kant's disinterested pleasure.
- Philosophy as daily practice: Stoic exercises and Montaigne's self-examination.

## Starting points

Claude's suggestions to begin the shelf. Keep, replace, or delete them.

- **Plato, *Timaeus***. A cosmos built by a craftsman from number and proportion: the shared root of Hermetic cosmology and Renaissance architecture.
- **Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations***. Private notes written for himself alone: in effect, the second brain of an emperor.
- **Pierre Hadot, *Exercices spirituels et philosophie antique*** (1981; in English as *Philosophy as a Way of Life*, 1995). Ancient philosophy as a daily practice that transforms the one who practises it.

In a Roman house, the atrium was open to the sky. Rain fell through the roof into the *impluvium*, a basin that collected it. This room works the same way: whatever falls into your mind is caught here, before it can evaporate.

## Rules of the room

- **Capture, never sort.** Sorting at the moment of capture is the friction that kills second brains.
- **Anything is welcome:** a sentence, a link, a photo description, half a thought, a voice note transcribed.
- **The room empties weekly.** At the weekly review, every item moves to its room or is discarded.

To capture, tell Claude `Capture: …`, `Запиши: …`, or `Note : …`.

**Status: intake in progress.** One dimension is set; the rest come from part II of the intake. Your position on each dimension is set from your [intake](/speculum/intake) answers and revised at each seasonal review, using evidence from the [decision journal](/speculum/decisions).

## Settings

- **Intensity:** `firm`. Claude argues the counter-case once, clearly, then yields. The other settings are `gentle` (Claude only notes the counter-case) and `relentless` (Claude argues it and raises it again at the next review).
- **Active-project cap:** 5.
- **Mode per dimension:** `mirror` (Claude thinks as you do), `counter` (Claude takes the other pole), or `off`.

*Venustas* and *utilitas* are two of the three qualities Vitruvius demanded of every building: beauty and usefulness. The third, *firmitas* (soundness), is the job of the whole palace.

## Rules of engagement

1. Values and the Lararium are always mirrored, never countered.
2. Every counterweight passage begins with **Counterweight:**.
3. One round, then yield. Once you decide, Claude executes fully and does not relitigate.
4. You can override any setting in the moment: `Mirror me` or `Counter me`.

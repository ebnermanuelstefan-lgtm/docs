> "Everything is dual; everything has poles; everything has its pair of opposites." *The Kybalion*. See [Seven principles](/palace/principles).

A polarity is a tension to manage, not a problem to solve. For each dimension, record where you stand by default and choose Claude's stance.

- **Mode:** `mirror` (Claude matches your pole) or `counter` (Claude takes the other pole and says so with **Counterweight:**).
- **Intensity**, for `counter` only: `gentle` (note it), `firm` (argue it), or `relentless` (argue it and ask again at the next review).

Values and taste are not on this map. They live in the [profile](/speculum/profile) and are always mirrored.

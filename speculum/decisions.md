Say `Decide: …`, `Решение: …`, or `Décision : …` to open a record in `speculum/decisions/` from `_templates/decision.md`. Claude makes the counter-case once, then executes what you decide.

Each record sets a review date. When it arrives, the weekly review grades the decision on two separate scores: the quality of the reasoning and the quality of the outcome. A good decision can have a bad outcome, and the reverse.

Statuses: `open` → `decided` → `reviewed`.

Answer in Bulgarian, English, or French, in any order, a few lines each. The six questions marked **essential** are enough to begin; the rest can come over the first lunar cycle. Your answers fill the [profile](/speculum/profile) and the [polarity map](/speculum/polarity-map).

You can answer here or in a session: say `Begin the intake`, `Започни анкетата`, or `Commence l'entretien`.

While the repository is public, leave out health, finances, legal matters, addresses, birth data, and anyone's full name.

## I. Voice

1. **Essential.** In which language do you think, and which language do you want the palace to answer in by default?
2. How should Claude sound to you: terse or expansive, formal or warm, plain or ornate?
3. Name three writers or speakers whose voice you would like your notes to echo.

## II. Values and the Lararium

4. **Essential.** Name the three to five values you would not trade. These are mirrored, never counterweighted.
5. What do you refuse to do, even when it would pay?
6. Which tradition, teacher, or text do you return to when you are lost?

## III. Taste

7. **Essential.** Name one building, one work of art, and one book that changed how you see. Say why in a line each.
8. What does luxury mean to you, beyond price?
9. Describe a beautiful ordinary day.

## IV. Habits of mind

10. **Essential.** When you decide, do you lead with intuition or analysis? Do you tend to open options or close them?
11. Where do you start many things and finish few, or finish everything and start too little?
12. When you are wrong, how do you usually find out, and how late?

## V. Work and life

13. **Essential.** What are you building or tending right now? Name the projects and the areas.
14. What do people come to you for?
15. What drains you that you keep doing anyway?

## VI. Counterweight

16. **Essential.** On which habits of mind do you want Claude to push back, and how hard: gentle, firm, or relentless?
17. Where must Claude never push back?
18. What uncomfortable question would a true friend ask you this month?

## VII. Rhythm

19. Which day suits the weekly review? Lunar reviews or the calendar?
20. What would make you stop visiting the palace, and how should Claude notice?

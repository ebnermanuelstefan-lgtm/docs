The palace is built, but it holds nothing of you yet. Your answers become your [profile](/speculum/profile) and your [polarity map](/speculum/polarity-map): the instructions that tell Claude where to mirror you and where to push against you.

**How to answer.** Use any language, any length, in any order. Paste your answers into a Claude session ("Here are my intake answers: …"), or write them under each question and commit. Bullet points are enough. Skip what you like and return later.

**If you only have ten minutes,** answer the six questions marked ★.

## I. Your world: what the palace must hold

1. ★ What are the main domains of your life right now? Think of work or vocation, ventures, study, creative practice, family, health, money, and home.
2. ★ Name up to five things you are actively trying to finish, with a rough date for each.
3. You have named architecture, art, philosophy, Hermeticism, astrology, classical luxury, and fine living. What is missing? Which are professional, and which are private passions?
4. Where does your information live today? Think of phone notes, messages to yourself, Gmail, Google Drive, photos and screenshots, voice memos, books with marginalia, and saved posts.
5. ★ What do you most often lose or forget? Ideas, quotations, names, promises to others, appointments, or where you saved something?

## II. How your mind moves: the polarity map

6. ★ When a new idea arrives, what do you usually do with it? Act at once, write it down and forget it, let it ripen, or talk it through with someone?
7. Do you start more than you finish? Give an honest ratio.
8. Big picture or detail: at which altitude do you get bored?
9. Do you decide fast, by intuition, or slowly, by analysis? Which do you regret more: rushed decisions or delayed ones?
10. Do you think in images, in words, in spaces, or through your hands?
11. When are you sharpest, and what reliably derails you?
12. What would someone who knows you well name as your blind spot?
13. ★ How should I push back? Directly ("this is a mistake") or through questions? Gently, firmly, or relentlessly? And where should I never push: what is yours alone to decide?

## III. Your voice: what I should mirror

14. ★ Paste three to five samples of your own writing: messages, notes, or posts, in any of your languages.
15. Which language do you think in for which subject? For example: feelings in Bulgarian, business in English, aesthetics in French.
16. What do you dislike in writing? Jargon, emoji, flattery, padding, or something else?

## IV. Practicalities

17. Where will you use the palace most: phone, laptop, or both? Do you already use Obsidian, Notion, Apple Notes, or another tool?
18. This repository is currently **public**. Shall we make it private? Which kinds of information must never be stored here, even then?
19. How much time will you honestly give it? The default is 10 seconds per capture, 5 minutes a day, and 25 minutes a week.

## V. Optional: the symbolic layer

20. If you want astrology as a lens in the Speculum and in the review calendar, give your birth date, time, and place. Share these only after the repository is private. If you have taken a personality inventory, share the results too. The Big Five has the strongest research base; MBTI, the Enneagram, or Human Design are welcome as self-descriptions.

## What happens next

1. Claude writes your profile and fills in the polarity map from your answers.
2. You correct both. Your corrections outrank Claude's inferences.
3. Claude seeds the Atelier and Tablinum from questions 1 and 2, and proposes how to bring in what you named in question 4.
4. The first weekly review takes place seven days later. The polarity map is retuned at the first seasonal review: the winter solstice, 21 December 2026.

☿ **Mercury's room: thought and connection.** The Hortus is a *Zettelkasten*. Each note holds one idea of your own, titled as a claim: "Proportion is a form of memory", not "Proportion".

Say `Plant: …` to sow a seed from `_templates/idea.md`. Claude fills the **Against it** section. Ideas grow from `seed` to `sapling` to `evergreen`. They are never archived, only matured.

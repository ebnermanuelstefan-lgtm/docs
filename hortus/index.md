The *hortus conclusus*, the enclosed garden, was the private garden of the medieval cloister and the villa. This one grows your own thinking. Only your ideas live here. Quotations and references stay on the shelves of the [Bibliotheca](/bibliotheca); your response to them is planted here.

## How ideas grow

| Stage | Meaning | Moves on when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seed** | A raw thought, in a sentence or two | You return to it a second time |
| **Sapling** | Argued: why you believe it, what speaks against it | It connects to at least two other ideas |
| **Evergreen** | Stable, linked, and in your own words | Rarely; it gets revised, not replaced |

## Rules of the garden

- **One idea per note.** If a note holds two ideas, split it.
- **Title it as a claim:** "Luxury is the removal of friction", not "Luxury".
- **Every idea has an opponent.** Each note contains an **Against it** section, written by Claude as counterweight.
- **Link generously.** An idea's value is in its connections. Each note names what it grows from and what it leads to.

To plant an idea, tell Claude `Plant: …`.

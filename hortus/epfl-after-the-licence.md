## The idea

A full architecture degree at EPFL was missed. It can still be reached later, through a master's or doctorate, after the Bulgarian degree and the licence to practise (печат).

## Why I believe it

The door is not closed. It has moved further along the path.

## Against it

**Counterweight:** the first goal is the Bulgarian degree in 2028, and the backlog comes before anything else. Keep this as a long-term option, and don't let it compete with the current path.

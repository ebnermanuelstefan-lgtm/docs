## The idea

I want to own a business. I do not yet know which kind. Until I do, it is an idea to grow, not a project to start.

## Why I believe it

I love sales and working with people, I love offering something high-end, and I am training as an architect. Those three meet somewhere.

## Against it

**Counterweight:** an open question can absorb endless thinking without producing a step. Directions to test one at a time, after the first wave:

1. Luxury interior design for high-end private clients.
2. Specification sales for luxury furniture and lighting brands, selling to architects and designers.
3. Retail and boutique interior design for fashion houses.
4. Styling and organisation for luxury homes.

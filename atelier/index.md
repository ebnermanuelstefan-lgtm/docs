The atelier is the workshop, where things are made and finished. A note belongs here only if it can end.

## What makes a project

- **An outcome**: what exists, or is true, when it is done.
- **A horizon**: a date, even a rough one.
- **A next action**: one concrete, physical step.

Without an outcome it is an area; put it in the [Tablinum](/tablinum). Without a horizon it is a wish; plant it in the [Hortus](/hortus) until you are ready.

The number of active projects is capped by your [polarity map](/speculum/polarity-map). The default cap is five. Starting a sixth means pausing one.

To open a project, tell Claude `New project: …`.

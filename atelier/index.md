♂︎ **Mars's room: action.** A project has a clear outcome and a horizon date. Without both, it is an area or an idea.

Say `New project: …` to open one from `_templates/project.md`. Each active project keeps one concrete next action, checked at every weekly review. Finished or abandoned projects descend to the [Hypogeum](/hypogeum).
